Qazaq Blend

This is a branding project where I created designs for canned milk tea inspired by Kazakh culture. This is a self-initiated project created as a further development of my freshman year assignment from Parsons. The goal was to create a brand identity for a Kazakh company that created culture-themed produce. "Qazaq Blend" is a company, inspired by the beauty of local tradition, created mostly canned beverages that aim to bring a sense to nostalgia to older generations and a sense of belonging to the younger ones. The brand is focused on revival of Kazakh traditions in a more modern, minimalistic setting to infuse the younger generations with love for their culture. The brand will be targeting teenagers and young adults, marketed towards a more carefree, younger audience.

Tools: Adobe Illustrator, Procreate. | Role: Product Designer, Illustrator | Year: 2023
