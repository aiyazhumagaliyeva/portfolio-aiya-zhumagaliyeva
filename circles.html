Circles - zine

"Slow Down, Look Around" - the zine that encourages its readers to slow down their days and notice the life around them. Combining 2 philosophical passages ("Circles" by Emerson and Plato's "Theory of Forms") and a poem by Rilke ("Widening Circles"), the zine unfolds, showcasing a collection of images of circles found all around the busy NYC. This project was an exercise in experimental editorial design.

Tools: Indesign, Photoshop | Role: Designer, Editor | Year: 2023
